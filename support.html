Frequently asked questions and help

How do I get a new quote?

Swipe left on the main screen. To go back to a previous quote, swipe right.

How do I share a quote?

Swipe up or long-press on the quote to open the share screen. Choose a card style and tap Share.

How do I save a quote?

Tap the bookmark icon in the bottom-right corner. Long-press it to add the quote to a specific collection.

How do I change my quote preferences?

Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner to open Settings. You can select which categories inspire you, choose your font, and configure notifications.

What is the sparkle icon?

Tap it to ask for quotes about a specific topic. Type a topic like "overcoming fear" and QuotedAI will find or generate a relevant quote.

What does the AI indicator mean?

A small sparkle next to the author name means the quote was generated by on-device AI. You can turn this off in Settings under "AI-generated quotes."

How do location-based quotes work?

In Settings, go to Places and add locations like your gym or office. When you arrive, you'll get a notification with a quote matching that place's vibe. This requires "Always" location permission for background monitoring.

What data does QuotedAI collect?

None. Everything stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I use QuotedAI with Siri?

Say "Give me a quote from QuotedAI" or "What's my quote of the day from QuotedAI." You can also create Shortcuts automations.
