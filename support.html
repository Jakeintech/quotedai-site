Support

For Quoted 3.0 on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Write to quotedaiapp@gmail.com. Include your device and iOS version, and what you expected to happen. A reply usually comes within two working days.

How do I get a new line?

Swipe left on the line, or tap the arrow in the bar at the bottom. Swipe right to go back.

How do I save or share a line?

Tap the bookmark to save it. Tap the share button to make a card for Instagram Stories, a post or a message, or to send the line itself.

What does the gilt mark mean?

The line was matched word for word against a public-domain source. Tap the mark to read the passage around it and open the full text. Lines without the mark are attributed in the usual way and have not yet been traced.

Why did Quoted choose this line?

Tap the question mark in the bar. Quoted shows the reasons: the hour, the themes you have chosen and saved, and, only if you allowed them, your activity and places.

How do I ask for something specific?

Open Explore and use Ask. Describe what you need in your own words, such as "something about starting over". Quoted searches its library on your device.

My daily line arrives at the wrong time, or not at all.

Open Profile, then Reminders, and check the time. Then check iOS Settings, Notifications, Quoted, and that Allow Notifications is on. Focus modes can hold notifications back.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Quoted. Profile, Widgets shows every size.

How do I restore Quoted Pro on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the paywall from Profile, and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel in iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Quoted Pro. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I think an attribution is wrong.

Write to us with the line and what you know about it. A line that cannot be traced is removed.

How do I turn off author portraits?

Profile, then "Author portraits from Wikipedia". With it off, Quoted makes no requests to Wikipedia.
