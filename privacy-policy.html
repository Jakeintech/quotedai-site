Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026. Applies to Quoted 3.0 and later.

The short version. Quoted has no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking software. Choosing a line, searching, and explaining a choice happen on your device. We, the developer, receive none of your data. A few features talk to Apple or to Wikipedia, each listed below with what is sent, and each can be switched off.

What stays on your device

Stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple Watch:

  • Your settings: themes, reading preferences, reminder times, and the optional name you give the app.
  • Lines you save, your collections and your own categories.
  • Which lines you have seen, so the app does not repeat itself. Older history is pruned automatically.
  • What you write in Dawn Line, Evening Echo, Top Three and the journal.
  • Your Dawn Line count and similar tallies.

This data is included in your device backup if you back up your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Permissions, all optional

Quoted works fully without any of these. Each is asked for only when you turn on the feature that uses it.

Notifications

To deliver your daily line at the time you choose. These are scheduled on your device; there is no push server.

Apple Health (read only)

Active energy and workouts, read on your device to tell whether you are at rest or have been moving, so the line suits your energy. Quoted never writes to Health, never stores what it reads, and never sends it anywhere. Health data is not used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Location

Used for up to five places you choose (for example, a gym or an office), so a line can arrive when you get there. iOS watches those places; only the places you set are stored, on your device. If you allow it, your approximate location is also sent to Apple Weather as described above.

Calendar and Reminders

Calendar: read on your device to notice a busy day or a meaningful date. Events are not stored or sent. Reminders: if you link a reminder to a focus session, Quoted marks it done when the session ends.

Apple Music

The genre of what is playing, read on your device to match the line to the mood. Not stored or sent.

Photos (add only)

To save a share card to your library when you ask. Quoted cannot see your existing photos. Choosing a photo for a card uses Apple's picker, which shows Quoted only the photo you pick.

On-device intelligence

Ask, "why this one", and the Practice tools use Apple's on-device models and on-device language features. Your requests and writing are processed on your device and are not sent to us or to any server. Quoted does not write lines and attribute them to real people; text it generates for you, such as a personal mantra, is labelled as yours and credited to no one.

Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight and widgets

Quoted can appear in Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight, and its widgets, Live Activities and Watch app read the same on-device data through a shared app container. Apple's own privacy terms cover Siri. Nothing is sent to us.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or email collection.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or attribution software of any kind.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of data.

Children

Quoted is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone. Some optional content is held behind an age confirmation.

Your choices and rights

Everything Quoted holds about you is on your devices and in your own iCloud, so you can see it in the app, export your journal from the app, and delete it by deleting the app and, if you use sync, the app's data in iCloud settings. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf, but you are welcome to ask.

Changes

If a future version changes what is described here, this page will change in the same release and the date above will move.
