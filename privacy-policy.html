QuotedAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

Overview

QuotedAI is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. Everything happens on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

QuotedAI does not collect:

  • Personal information (email, phone number, or account credentials)
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Any data from third-party SDKs

QuotedAI contains zero third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks.

On-Device Data

The following data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Preferences — Your selected categories, font, theme, and notification settings
  • Bookmarks & Collections — Quotes you save and organize
  • Quote History — Which quotes you have seen (used to avoid repetition)
  • Mood Check-Ins — Your daily mood entries
  • Display Name — The name you optionally provide during onboarding

This data is stored using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults and App Group containers) and is included in your iCloud device backup if you have iCloud Backup enabled.

HealthKit Data

If you enable the "Match quotes to your energy" feature, QuotedAI reads recent workout completions and active energy burned data from Apple HealthKit. This data is:

  • Read-only — QuotedAI never writes to HealthKit
  • Processed entirely on your device — used to determine your current energy level (calm, steady, or intense)
  • Never stored, logged, or transmitted — only the resulting energy level classification is retained temporarily in memory
  • Used solely to select contextually relevant quotes

In compliance with Apple's HealthKit guidelines, health data is never shared with third parties, used for advertising, or stored outside the secure HealthKit container. You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health.

Location Data

If you enable location-based quote delivery, QuotedAI monitors up to 5 geofence regions that you define (e.g., your gym, office, or favorite cafe). This data is:

  • Defined by you — QuotedAI only monitors places you explicitly add
  • Processed on-device by iOS — geofence monitoring is handled by the operating system, not by our code
  • Never transmitted — your location is never sent to any server
  • Never stored — only the place names and coordinates you configure are saved locally

You can remove places or revoke location permission at any time in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services.

On-Device AI

On supported devices (iOS 26+), QuotedAI may use Apple's on-device Foundation Models to generate personalized quotes. This processing happens entirely on your device using Apple's privacy-preserving framework. No prompts, inputs, or generated content are sent to any server.

Photo Library

When you choose to save a shared quote card image, QuotedAI requests write-only access to your photo library. QuotedAI does not browse, read, or access your existing photos.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, QuotedAI schedules local notifications on your device to deliver quotes at your preferred times. These are iOS local notifications — they do not involve any push notification server or external service.

Sharing

When you share a quote card via the iOS share sheet, the rendered image is passed to your chosen app (Instagram, iMessage, Snapchat, etc.) using Apple's built-in sharing mechanisms. Once content leaves QuotedAI, the receiving app's privacy policy governs that content. We do not track or log shares.

Third-Party Services

QuotedAI contains no third-party SDKs, frameworks, or services of any kind. The app is built exclusively with Apple frameworks (SwiftUI, WidgetKit, HealthKit, CoreLocation, ActivityKit, AVFoundation, UserNotifications).

Widgets, Watch App & Live Activities

QuotedAI's widgets, Apple Watch companion, and Live Activities access the same on-device data through a shared App Group container. No data is transmitted externally. Widget content is rendered locally.

Children's Privacy

QuotedAI does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app contains no user accounts, no social features, and no data collection of any kind. Mature content (if any) is gated behind an age confirmation screen.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, deleting the QuotedAI app removes all associated data. No data persists on any server because no data is ever sent to one.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Given that we collect no data, significant changes are unlikely.

Contact
