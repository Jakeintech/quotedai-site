Words someone actually said.

Most quote apps cannot tell you where a line came from, and a good share of what circulates was never said by the person named. Quoted keeps the record with the line.

Lines traced to their source carry a mark and the reference: the work, the chapter or letter, the year, the translator when there is one. Tap the mark to read the passage around it.

Some lines you will not find here.

The famous Harriet Tubman sayings are missing, because none can be traced to anything she said or wrote. “I disapprove of what you say, but I will defend to the death your right to say it” is not Voltaire. His biographer Evelyn Beatrice Hall wrote it in 1906.

Before you download.

Where do the quotes come from?

From books, letters, speeches and scriptures, chosen by hand. Lines marked as checked have been matched word for word against a public source, and the app shows you the reference and the surrounding passage. Lines without the mark are attributed in the usual way and have not yet been traced.

Does Quoted use AI to write quotes?

No. It finds and ranks real lines; it does not write one and put a person’s name on it. Affirmations in the app are written in-house and shown with no attribution.

How does it know my moment?

From the hour, the themes you choose and save, and, only if you allow them, your activity from Apple Health and the places you set. All of it is read on your device. Hold any line to see the reasons.

What does it cost?

Quoted is free to download and use. Quoted Pro is $4.99 a month, $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $59.99 once. Prices are for the United States.

What leaves my device?

Very little, and none of it to us. Sync uses your own iCloud. Author portraits and biographies come from Wikipedia, which you can turn off. Place search, weather and purchases go through Apple. The privacy policy lists every case.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later.

I think an attribution is wrong.

Tell us. Write to quotedaiapp@gmail.com with the line and what you know. A line that cannot be traced is removed.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Quoted Pro. If you cancel during the free trial you are not charged.
